Component library · DESIGN.md v0.5 · phase B gate

Eleven components, one primitive, no glow.

Every exhibit renders inside a 1920×1080 stage that's container-query-scaled to fit its card — the same dimensions and proportions slides will use in Phase C onwards. Inline styles are forbidden in slide markup; layout lives in base.css slots (.slide-content.*, .section-meta-top, .slider-slot) and in components.css composition blocks (.section-opener-hero, .agenda-block, .iso-stack-block, .path-caption). Idle motion (scatter drift, core breathe) is wired here; narrative motion is deferred.

§5

Frame chrome — section-opener with subsection slider

base.css

Every slide is framed by four corner ticks and a bottom meta line. Sections add a top-left section label with amber index, and a top-right subsection slider showing position within the section. When the slider sits in the top-right, the brand uses .with-slider to clear room. Sample below: opening of Step 05 · The Boundary, currently on subsection 2 of 4.

§6.5

Legibility scrim — mandatory under text-over-visual

--scrim radial · 86% → 0%

A soft radial pool of --l-bg at ~86% centre fading to 0% at the edge. Sits between the visual (orbs / scatter) and the headline, guaranteeing contrast on a weak projector. Required, not optional.
