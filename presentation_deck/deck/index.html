[01—The Diagnosis]
GHI Audit Approach
5-day on-site audit · Saudi Arabia · BACAB host context

The diagnosis came from field observation, not assumptions.

Observe Workflow
Trace Evidence
Discuss with Stakeholders
Map Control Gaps
Lifecycle-Control Diagnosis
Audit output → control gaps before quotation.
The audit was hosted by BACAB Consulting on-site at GHI in Saudi Arabia over 5 days. The method was deliberately convergent: three parallel investigations (observe workflow, trace evidence, discuss with stakeholders) fed one synthesis step (map control gaps), producing the lifecycle-control diagnosis. The concrete output — control gaps before quotation — is what 01.3 walks through.

Past RFQs · outcomes · lessons learned

Diagnosis → lifecycle control must come before quotation automation.
RFQ risk accumulates between "RFQ Received" and "Quotation Submitted" — the zone called "Before Quotation". Four control gaps live in that zone: Visibility, Coordination, Follow-up, Knowledge Reuse. Don't over-explain the Knowledge Reuse Gap here — it's slightly broader than the immediate pre-quotation workflow, but it sets up the lifecycle-intelligence framing in Section 02. Bottom line lands the pivot: lifecycle control must come before quotation automation.
