03

The Strategic Pivot

Original Ask → New Direction

GHI initially requested help to automate BOQ creation using AI. After deep audit, this was determined to be 100% infeasible — the estimation process is entirely expertise-driven, with no extractable rules, no replicable formulas, and no structured knowledge base. The BOQ's 36 interconnected sheets are filled by senior estimators whose judgment cannot be codified.

New approach: Analyze the full RFQ lifecycle end-to-end — from reception to executive reporting — and build an AI-powered RFQ Lifecycle Intelligence Platform that eliminates every pain point, bottleneck, and communication failure surrounding the estimation process, without attempting to replace the estimators' expertise.

04

Identified Pain Points

Combined findings from the on-site audit and the subsequent analytical review of the process and workbook.

PP-01

Inter-Departmental Communication Breakdown

Departments operate in complete silos. No centralized system tracks RFQ status across Estimation, Engineering, Production, QC, or Procurement. Projects have been lost because critical information (e.g., a component deemed infeasible by Engineering) never reached Estimation in time. A single unfeasible piece can kill an entire project — and the signal doesn't propagate.

Audit Finding
PP-02

Manual Client Follow-Up & Communication

All client interactions — reminders, responses, follow-ups, deadline tracking — are handled manually. This has directly caused lost projects due to missed deadlines, forgotten follow-ups, and delayed responses. No automation, no alerts, no audit trail of client communication.

Audit Finding
PP-03

Zero Executive Visibility — No BI / KPI Layer

GHI and Albassam leadership have no dashboards, no KPIs, no real-time view of the estimation pipeline. They depend entirely on verbal updates or direct questioning. Cannot answer: Where is RFQ X now? What's our win rate? Which clients are most profitable? What's our pipeline value?

Audit Finding
PP-04

No Learning from Historical RFQs

Every new RFQ is treated as a completely fresh exercise. No institutional memory, no pattern recognition, no leverage from past bids. Historical pricing, win/loss outcomes, client behavior patterns, and similar project data are never systematically reused.

Audit Finding
PP-05

Late Discovery of Infeasibility

Projects can reach advanced stages before a critical blocker is discovered — a mandatory subvendor who can't deliver, a material that's unavailable, or a specification that can't be met. These late-stage discoveries are extremely costly in wasted man-hours and lost opportunity.

Audit Finding
PP-06

Excel Workbook — Single Point of Failure

The entire financial model for multi-million SAR projects lives in a single Excel file with 36 interconnected sheets. No version control, no audit trail, no concurrent editing. A broken formula in one sheet silently cascades across the entire estimation. Two people cannot work on the same file simultaneously.

Analytical Finding
PP-07

Undocumented Feasibility Expertise

The 10–20 minute feasibility gut-check by the department manager is never recorded or structured. The reasoning behind every go/no-go decision is lost. This expertise cannot be analyzed, learned from, or transferred — it walks out the door when the manager retires.

Analytical Finding
PP-08

IB/IF Classification Not Enforcing Downstream Workflows

The IB (Internal Bid) vs. IF (Internal Fabrication) classification determines the entire downstream path, but no system enforces the workflow implications. Different classifications should trigger different department notifications, checklists, and approval gates — currently they don't.

Analytical Finding
PP-09

Unmanaged Subvendor & Supplier Risk

The supplier market is highly volatile. Certain clients mandate specific suppliers (RVL for Aramco). But there's no system tracking supplier lead times, historical reliability, price trends, or availability. Estimators call/email and wait. No supplier intelligence layer exists.

Analytical Finding
PP-10

Odoo ERP — Underutilized Ghost System

Odoo is deployed but barely used. Likely causes duplicated data entry (Excel + Odoo) or worse — data only in Excel with Odoo perpetually out of date. The ERP is not fulfilling its core purpose, creating data fragmentation across the organization.

Analytical Finding
PP-11

No Data-Driven Bid Strategy (Awarded vs. Focused Bids)

GHI makes strategic choices about which bids to focus on (focused bids) vs. standard submissions. But this decision is intuition-based, not data-driven. No analysis of win probability by client, project type, size, or competitive landscape exists to inform bid prioritization.

Analytical Finding
PP-12

Standards & Compliance Not Triggering Cost Implications

ASME, TEMA, FATI requirements aren't just checkboxes — they trigger specific cost lines (ASME stamp, NB registration, FEA), specific supplier mandates, and specific QC/QA workflows. Currently, these implications may be discovered mid-process rather than flagged automatically at RFQ intake.

Analytical Finding

PILLAR 1

Workflow & Tracking Engine

Real-time cross-departmental visibility into every RFQ, with automated routing based on project classification.

Live RFQ status across all departments
Role-based permissions (worker → lead → manager → C-suite)
IB/IF classification triggers different workflow paths
Dependency tracking with early blocker alerts
ASME/TEMA/FATI auto-flagging at intake
Excel workbook version tracking & anomaly detection
PILLAR 2

Client Communication Automation

AI-assisted client engagement with smart suggestions and human-in-the-loop approval before every action.

Auto-generated follow-up drafts & reminders
Deadline tracking with escalation alerts
Unified inbox: Email + SAP Ariba portal monitoring
Lead/worker confirms before any outbound action
Full communication audit trail per RFQ
PILLAR 3

Executive Intelligence & BI

Real-time dashboards giving GHI and Albassam leadership full visibility into performance and pipeline.

Pipeline funnel: RFQs received → estimated → submitted → won
Win rate, hit rate, and margin KPIs
Client segmentation by profitability & volume
Department performance & bottleneck analytics
Focused bid vs. standard bid outcome comparison
Shop load & capacity utilization
PILLAR 4

Historical Learning & Prediction

AI engine that learns from past RFQs to accelerate decisions and reduce risk on future bids.

Similar project matching from historical data
Preliminary cost range (not detailed — ballpark only)
Client win probability scoring
Supplier price trend tracking & risk scoring
Feasibility pattern recognition from past go/no-go decisions
Bid strategy optimization: where to invest effort
