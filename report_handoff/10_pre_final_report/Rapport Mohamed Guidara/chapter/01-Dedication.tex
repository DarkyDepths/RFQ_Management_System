With a heart full of gratitude, I first thank God, whose grace carried me through doubt, fatigue, and uncertainty, and gave me the strength to bring this work to its end.\\[0.55cm]
{\fontsize{25pt}{25pt}\selectfont To} \textbf{my beloved parents, Hajer and Naceur,}\\[0.12cm]
for the quiet sacrifices, the prayers that walked ahead of me, and the love that held me steady when I could not hold myself. Every page of this work carries something of you.\\[0.55cm]
{\fontsize{25pt}{25pt}\selectfont To} \textbf{my dear brother, Amir, and his wife Yosr,}\\[0.12cm]
for being my shelter, my confidants, and the familiar warmth that made even the heaviest days feel lighter.\\[0.55cm]
{\fontsize{25pt}{25pt}\selectfont To} \textbf{my dear sister, Ons, and her husband Omar,}\\[0.12cm]
for your tenderness, your faith in me, and the reassurance you gave me whenever my own certainty became fragile.\\[0.55cm]
{\fontsize{25pt}{25pt}\selectfont To} \textbf{all my family and to the friends who became family,}\\[0.12cm]
for standing beside me through every uncertainty, every late night, and every small victory.
